Usability audit

Antz Onboarding Kit
Web edition v0.1

What works, what gets in a prospect's way, and how to make the kit more intuitive and more beautiful.

Date24 September 2026
ScopeHome page, 32 module pages, search
AudienceProspective clients
Findings19 (5 high)
Summary

The foundation is right. The next step is where it falls down.

Verdict

The kit already does the hard part. It turns a 46-slide training deck into a short home page with real product screens one click away. A prospect can see what Antz is within seconds. The problems start when they try to go deeper or take the next step. On a phone, the product tour gives no visible feedback. Four modules show stock photos dressed up as app screens. One module shows the wrong module's screen. The only way to get in touch is an email link. The brand green also fails contrast on every primary button. All of the high-severity issues can be fixed in days, not weeks.

Strengths

What already works (keep it)

Short home page. Seven sections instead of 46 slides. A prospect sees the platform, one workflow and the areas of work without scrolling through a manual.
Real product, click-driven. Each feature shows its actual screen, on the device it runs on. That matches the earlier research ruling that the tour should be click-driven, not scroll-driven.
Search in the hero, with live results across modules and features. The rotating placeholder shows the range of what's there.
One source of content. Every page renders from content.json, so a fact is fixed in one place. This was the root cause named in framing.md.
Honest content. Unfinished modules are hidden, no unreliable total is printed, and the Housing hierarchy error is corrected.
Responsive, with reduced motion respected. It works at 390, 1024 and 1440px. The parallax and animations switch off for visitors who have reduced motion turned on.
Scope and method

How this audit was done

A heuristic review of the kit as built, from the point of view of a prospect: a curator, vet lead or director who has never used Antz and has five minutes.

What was reviewed

  • Home page: hero, search, Why Antz, featured workflow, Explore, Getting started, call to action, FAQ, footer
  • All 32 visible module pages, content checked against content.json
  • Search: queries, empty state, keyboard control
  • Viewports: 1440px desktop, 1024px tablet, 390px phone (Chrome 153)

Checks performed

  • Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, applied to five prospect journeys
  • WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast calculated for every text and background pair in the style sheet; keyboard paths; semantics
  • Content audit: missing or mismatched screenshots, duplicate modules, copy length and pattern
  • Asset weight on disk

Severity

High · 5Misleads a prospect, blocks a core task, or fails WCAG AA on the main path. Fix before sharing.
Medium · 10Slows people down, causes doubt, or weakens the case for Antz.
Low · 4Polish and consistency.

Limits

  • No sessions with real prospects yet. Scores are an expert estimate. A five-task test plan in the Roadmap section will confirm them.
  • Screen readers were not tested live. Semantic issues were found by reading the code.
  • Page speed was estimated from file sizes, not measured on a hosted build.
Findings

19 findings, by prospect journey

1 · Understanding a module

F1

On mobile, tapping a feature changes a screen that is out of view

High
Left: the screen sits above the list. Right: after tapping step 4, only its caption is visible.
Seen
At phone width the device preview sits above the feature list. Once a visitor scrolls down to the features, tapping one updates a screen they can no longer see. The step highlights, but nothing visible happens.
Why
The core interaction of the kit gives no feedback on the device most prospects will open a shared link on (visibility of system status).
Fix
At 900px and below, show the screenshot inline under the selected step, as an accordion. Alternatively, use a small sticky preview that stays on screen while scrolling.
Effort
Half a day
F2

Four modules show a stock photo inside a phone frame, as if it were the app

High
Lab: a stock lab photo cropped into the phone, used for all 8 features.
Seen
Lab (8 features), Diet (6), Pharmacy · Web (8) and Compliance (9) each have one image, and it's a stock photo. The page shows it in a phone frame for every feature.
Why
A prospect reads the frame as "this is the product". It looks broken at best and misleading at worst, in the modules that matter most to vets and compliance leads.
Fix
When a module has no real screens, drop the device and show the features as cards with the photo as a header. Mark these modules needs-capture and commission the screens.
Effort
2 hours plus capture time
F3

The featured workflow opens on a dimmed screen

Medium
Seen
The first Animal Transfer screen shows a modal over a grey scrim, so the page's main product image looks disabled.
Why
This is the only product image a visitor sees without clicking. It should be the most confident screen in the kit.
Fix
Default to a clean, busy screen, such as the approval view (p14-transfer-2.jpg). Let the dimmed modal appear only on step 1.
Effort
15 minutes
F4

Feature descriptions stay hidden until each step is clicked

Low
Seen
Only the selected step shows its description, so scanning a nine-feature module like Compliance takes nine clicks.
Fix
Keep the progressive disclosure, but add a one-line summary per step, or a "Show all" toggle for people who prefer to read than click.
Effort
1 hour

2 · Finding the right module

F5

Three treatment modules are near copies, and Deworming shows the Vaccination screen

High
Vaccination (left) and Deworming (right). The Deworming phone is titled "Vaccination" and lists "Stopped Vaccine" and "Schedule Vaccination".
Seen
Vaccination, Deworming and Supplements share the same six features and layout. The Deworming screenshot belongs to Vaccination. Four App/Web pairs (HIMS, Egg, Reports, Pharmacy) also appear as separate modules.
Why
Repeats make the catalogue feel padded and leave people thinking "haven't I seen this?". The wrong screenshot is a factual error a vet will spot straight away.
Fix
Replace the Deworming capture now. Merge the three into one "Scheduled treatments" module with a type switch, and merge each App/Web pair into one module with an App | Web toggle. That takes the catalogue from 32 to about 24 modules, in line with IA-MAPPING.md.
Effort
1 day (content and a small UI toggle)
F6

The area tabs hide most of the catalogue, and Records looks empty

Medium
Seen
Only one area is visible at a time, and it opens on the thinnest one: Records, with two cards and a large empty space. Its description mentions "chat", but Chat is hidden. The selected area isn't in the URL, so it can't be shared.
Why
The first impression of the catalogue is "small". Recognition beats recall: prospects can't compare areas they can't see.
Fix
Open on the richest area (Animal Records & Movement) or on an "All" view. Put the area in the URL (#/area/medical). Generate each area's description from the modules actually shown.
Effort
3 hours
F7

Card copy was written for training, not for a first look

Medium
Seen
28 of 37 intros start with "The … Module". Cards cut them off after two lines, so they read like "The Notes Module centralises logging of daily updates, observations, and incident…". The median intro is 32 words.
Why
Low information scent. The first words, which carry the most weight, are spent on the module's name, which the card title already shows.
Fix
Add a tagline field of 12 words or fewer, outcome first. For example: "Every observation logged, linked to the animal, visible to the right team". Keep the long intro for the module page.
Effort
Half a day (copywriting)
F8

Search is only on the home page, and going back loses your place

Medium
Seen
Module pages have no search. Pressing Back from a module returns to the top of the home page, not to the Explore section the visitor came from.
Why
Browsing becomes a loop of scroll, click, back, scroll again (user control and freedom).
Fix
Add a compact search to the header on every page, opened with / or ⌘K. Save and restore the scroll position and area tab when going back to the home page.
Effort
Half a day

3 · First impression

F9

The headline competes with the tiger's face

Medium
Seen
With the text centred, the headline sits across the tiger's eye and muzzle. Readability depends on a strong dark overlay that also dulls the photo.
Why
Two focal points in the same spot. The hero loses both its impact and its clarity.
Fix
Keep the centred layout, but either crop the photo so the face sits above the headline, or choose an image with open space in the centre. A split layout was tried earlier and kept the face clear.
Effort
1 hour
F10

On a phone, the hero fills the whole first screen

Medium
Seen
At 390px, the photo, headline, search and five stacked chips take up about 840px. Nothing on the first screen shows there is more below. The chips are 32px tall, under the 44px touch target that Apple and Google recommend.
Fix
On phones, show the area chips as one horizontal scrolling row, make them 40 to 44px tall, and shorten the hero so the top of "Why Antz" peeks in. Add a sticky "Book a walkthrough" bar after the first scroll.
Effort
2 hours
F11

The rotating placeholder never stops, and it can't be acted on

Medium
Seen
The suggestion changes every 2.4 seconds for as long as the page is open. Pressing Enter on "Search Housing" does nothing, because the field is actually empty.
Why
WCAG 2.2.2 asks that moving content lasting more than 5 seconds can be paused. Visitors also expect a suggestion to work.
Fix
Pause on hover and after three full cycles. Slow it to about 3.5 seconds. On focus, pressing Enter or Tab fills in the current suggestion and searches for it.
Effort
1 hour

4 · Taking the next step

F12

"Book a walkthrough" is only an email link

High
Seen
All three calls to action open mailto:hello@antz.systems. On a work laptop with no mail app set up, or on a shared device, the click does nothing, and there is no confirmation either way.
Why
This is the kit's single goal, and its most likely point of failure.
Fix
Link to a short form (name, organisation, number of sites, preferred time) or to a scheduling page. Show a confirmation state. Keep the email address visible, with a copy button, as a fallback.
Effort
Half a day plus a form endpoint
F13

There is no proof for the claims

Medium
Seen
"Why Antz" lists six benefits ("Secure by design", "Real-time updates") with no numbers, customers, quotes or certifications behind them.
Why
A prospect is looking for reasons to believe. Unbacked benefit cards read like marketing and are easy to skip.
Fix
Reduce the six cards to three, each backed by one fact and one product crop. Add a strip with the number of sites, animals and species currently managed on Antz, and one short customer quote. This needs figures from Antz; nothing should be invented.
Effort
1 day once content exists
F14

The FAQ misses the questions buyers ask first

Low
Seen
The five FAQs cover what Antz is, devices, roles, sites and how to start. They don't cover pricing, time to go live, moving existing records into Antz, training, offline use, or where data is hosted.
Fix
Collect the top ten questions from the sales team and add answers they approve. Group them into Product, Setup and Security.
Effort
2 hours once answers exist

5 · Accessibility

F15

The brand green fails contrast on buttons and small text

High
White on #37BD69: 2.43:1
Seen
White on #37BD69 is 2.43:1, against a 4.5:1 minimum. This covers every "Book a walkthrough" button, the step numbers and the module icons. The darker green #2A9D55, used for eyebrows, links and "N features", is 3.47:1. The light grey #7A8684 is 3.77:1. Full table in the Appendix.
Fix
Add an action token --green-action: #27864B (4.57:1) for button fills and green text, and keep #37BD69 for large shapes and accents. Or keep the brand green on buttons with dark text #0E1715 (7.5:1). Change the light grey to #65716D (5.08:1).
Effort
1 hour (tokens only)
F16

Screen readers aren't told when the page changes, and search lacks the right roles

Medium
Seen
Moving between the home page and a module page swaps the content but doesn't move focus or announce the new page. The search box has no combobox role or aria-activedescendant, so arrowing through results is silent. The feature steps use role="tab" with no matching tab panels.
Fix
After each route change, move focus to the page's heading. Follow the ARIA 1.2 combobox pattern for search. Mark the steps as a listbox, or as buttons with aria-pressed.
Effort
Half a day

6 · Speed and consistency

F17

Screenshots are heavy

Medium
Seen
148 screenshots add up to 39 MB. The largest PNGs are 1.9 MB (p22-animal.png) and 1.6 MB. The hero JPEG is 0.7 MB. Images are lazy-loaded, but a module page on mobile data still takes seconds per screen.
Fix
Convert to WebP or AVIF at two sizes with srcset, aiming for 150 KB or less per screen and 250 KB or less for the hero. Preload the next step's screen when a step is hovered or tapped.
Effort
2 hours (script)
F18

Names and labels speak to staff, not prospects

Low
Seen
The header reads "Antz | Onboarding", but a prospect isn't onboarding yet. Module names mix styles ("Notes Module", "Housing", "Reports · App") and include a 45-character "Hospital Information Management System · App". Spelling mixes UK and US English (organisation, Familiarize).
Fix
Rename the header label to "Product tour". Drop "Module" from names and shorten HIMS to "Hospital". Standardise on UK English.
Effort
1 hour
F19

Icons mix two styles

Low
Seen
The icon set mixes outlined icons (add, edit, filter) with filled ones (notes, tags, report). At 15px, the Operations chip icon reads as a plain square.
Fix
Move to one family. The Figma file already uses Material Symbols (search_FILL0_wght400), so Material Symbols Rounded at weight 400 matches the product.
Effort
3 hours
Recommendations

Making it more intuitive

Prospects don't think in modules. They think about their job and their problems. These six changes move the kit from "here's everything we have" to "here's how Antz helps you".

1. Start with "Who are you?"

Below the hero, add four role cards: Curator, Veterinarian, Keeper, Director. Each opens a short path of three to five modules that matter to that role, with a line on why.

Cuts 32 choices down to four. Needs a role-to-module mapping from the product team.

2. Tell stories, not lists

Replace the single featured workflow with two or three short scenarios that cross modules: "Moving an animal between sites" (Transfer → Approvals → Security → Housing), "An animal falls ill", "An egg hatches".

IA-MAPPING.md §05 confirms the screens for the transfer scenario already exist.

3. A five-minute guided tour

Offer a "Take the tour" button that walks through five screens with Next and Back, a progress bar ("3 of 5") and a finish screen that leads to booking.

Gives a clear start, middle and end. Visitors can still leave the tour and explore freely.

4. Search everywhere, and forgiving

Put search in the header with ⌘K. Add synonyms (HIMS → hospital, "medicine" → Administer, "death" → Mortality) and show recent searches.

Prospects use their own words, not Antz's module names.

5. One module, both surfaces

Merge each App/Web pair and the three treatment modules. On a module page, an App | Web toggle switches the device frame and screens.

Fewer, richer modules. Shows the two-surface story visually instead of repeating it.

6. Remember and resume

Keep the visitor's place when they go back. Mark modules they've already viewed with a small tick, and offer "Continue where you left off" on return visits (stored in the browser only).

Lowers the effort of long evaluations that happen over several visits.

Recommendations

Making it more beautiful

The Figma "Help & Support" frame sets a strong direction: wildlife photography, white space, and the product on organic shapes. These changes push further in that direction and make it consistent.

Put the product in the hero

Float one real Antz phone screen beside the headline, on an organic blob like the Task Management frame in Figma. Photography brings the emotion; the screen proves the product.

A deliberate colour system

Three greens with set jobs: Brand #37BD69 for shapes and accents, Action #27864B for buttons and links, Tint #E8F6EE for backgrounds. Deep teal #1F515B is for dark sections only.

Screens as thumbnails on cards

Module cards currently show an icon and cut-off text. A cropped strip of the module's real screen at the top of each card gives instant recognition and variety.

Direct the photography

Pick one mood: natural light, green-leaning, the subject looking into the page, and space for text. Apply the same colour grade to every photo. Swap the chick used for "Mortality" for something calmer.

A tighter type scale

Six sizes: 56 / 40 / 28 / 20 / 16 / 14. Two weights: 600 for headings, 400 for body. Tabular numbers for counts. Sentence case for chips, which reads faster than spaced capitals.

Rhythm and motion

Alternate white, soft and dark sections. Use the fern-texture band from Figma for one dark "scenario" section. Keep motion to 140, 240 and 320ms on one easing curve, as in design-direction.md.

Five-task test with prospects

Run with five people who match the audience: curators, vet leads, operations managers. Sessions last 20 minutes, remote, thinking aloud. Run it after the "Now" fixes.

1"You manage three sites. Find out whether Antz can handle moving an animal between them."Measures: scenario findability, time
2"Find where you would record a vaccination and see what the screen looks like."Measures: search, module tour on mobile
3"What would your vets use Antz for every day?"Measures: role relevance, copy clarity
4"Is there anything here that makes you trust or doubt Antz?"Measures: credibility, proof gaps
5"Arrange a demo for your team."Measures: conversion path success

Track success rate per task, time on task, and a single ease rating (1 to 7) after each task. Five users typically surface most of the serious usability problems.
