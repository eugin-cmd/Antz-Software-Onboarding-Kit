Heuristic evaluation

Antz Onboarding
web experience

The current build, measured against Nielsen's ten usability heuristics, with suggestions and recommendations for what to change next.

Date25 September 2026
BuildLocal, antz-onboard/
AudienceProspective clients
Findings24 (6 major)
Summary

A polished tour whose content now lags behind its craft

Verdict

The interaction model is strong. Module cards open in place, the preview follows the visitor's tap on phones, Back restores their place, and every animation respects reduced motion. Most of the remaining problems are about what the page says and shows rather than how it behaves. Placeholder icons and two repeated photos make 32 modules look alike. Some screens are not the product. The staff-training copy speaks to the wrong reader. The one conversion path still depends on an email link. The motion layer has also grown to seven separate effects, which is more than a first-time visitor needs.

Recommended first moves

  1. Give every card its own identity. Replace placeholder icons and the two repeated photos with module-specific imagery. (H2.1, H6.1)
  2. Show only the real product. Swap the Deworming screen and remove stock photos from device frames. (H2.3)
  3. Rewrite the three kit sections for prospects. Drop "training", "kit" and "Module 03". (H2.2)
  4. Give booking a real destination with a confirmation state. (H5.1)
  5. Fix the action-green contrast and the module-page tour on phones. (H4.1, H1.1)

What is working well

Explore in place.Cards open a panel under their own row, so the grid never jumps and the visitor never loses context.
The preview follows the tap.On phones the screen appears under the selected feature, which fixes the most serious issue in the September audit.
State is kept.Back from a module restores the open card and exact scroll position. The logo resets cleanly.
Keyboard-friendly.Esc closes and returns focus to the card, arrow keys step through features, and chips and cards show the drawn border on focus.
Motion is considerate.Every effect switches off under reduced motion, and the card entrance is a quiet 12px fade-up.
A real brand signature.The green-to-teal drawn border ties cards, chips and panels into one recognisable system.
Method

How the evaluation was done

An expert review of the live local build, walking five prospect tasks at 1440px and 390px in Chrome 153, and checking each screen against Nielsen's ten heuristics.

Tasks walked

  • Understand what Antz is from the first screen
  • Find how Antz handles a vaccination, and see the screen
  • Compare two modules in the same area
  • Decide whether Antz fits a multi-site organisation
  • Book a walkthrough

Severity scale (Nielsen)

4 CatastrophicMust fix before release
3 MajorImportant to fix, high priority
2 MinorFix, lower priority
1 CosmeticFix if time allows

Limits

  • One evaluator. Heuristic reviews by a single person typically find around a third of usability problems, so a short test with prospects (see Validate) should follow.
  • Screen readers were not tested live. Timings were measured in the browser; page weight is from the network log (2.9 MB after a full tour).
  • The shared artifact link is an older version and was not evaluated.
Findings

24 findings across the ten heuristics

H1Visibility of system statusDoes the page show what is happening?

H1.1

On a phone, the module page tour still changes a screen that is out of view

3 Major
Step 4 selected; only the caption above is visible.
Seen
The card panels were fixed to show the screen under the tapped feature. The full module pages (#/m/…), which search results and "Open the full tour" lead to, still place the device above the list.
Why
The same action gives visible feedback in one view and none in the other. Search sends prospects straight into the broken one.
Fix
Reuse the panel's inline-preview behaviour on module pages at 900px and below.
H1.2

Card rows below the fold are invisible until scrolled to

2 Minor
Seen
Each area's cards start at zero opacity and fade up when the group enters the viewport. Link previews, thumbnails and full-page captures show empty gaps, and fast scrolling can outrun the reveal.
Fix
Render cards visible by default and only apply the fade when a group is about to enter view (add the hidden state just before, not on load).
H1.3

The rotating search suggestion never pauses and cannot be used

2 Minor
Seen
It changes every 2.4 seconds for as long as the page is open. Pressing Enter on "Search Housing" does nothing because the field is empty.
Fix
Pause after three cycles and on hover (WCAG 2.2.2). Let Enter or Tab accept the current suggestion.

H2Match between system and the real worldDoes it speak the prospect's language?

H2.1

Placeholder icons tell the wrong story

3 Major
Animal Management carries the egg icon, Tags Hub the hospital, Animal Transfer the necropsy clipboard.
Seen
16 of 32 cards use an icon picked from the set as a stand-in. Icons are read before titles, so each mismatch plants a wrong idea about the module.
Fix
Until the real icons exist, show the area's icon (paw, cross, egg, report) on cards without their own. It is generic but never wrong.
H2.2

The kit sections speak to trainees, not prospects

3 Major
Seen
"Why we run this training", "Objective of this kit", "Module 03 · Onboarding flow", the header label "Onboarding", and copy such as "get every team member confident" and "build confidence without supervision".
Why
A buyer is not yet on a training course. The words signal an internal document and weaken the pitch.
Fix
Reframe as outcomes: "What your team gets", "How rollout works". Rename the header label to "Product tour".
H2.3

Some "screens" are not the product

3 Major
Lab: a stock photo inside the phone frame.
Deworming: the phone shows the Vaccination screen.
Seen
Lab, Diet, Pharmacy · Web and Compliance show a stock photo framed as an app screen. Deworming shows Vaccination's menu, titled "Vaccination".
Why
The panels are the kit's proof. A vet or compliance lead will notice, and it undermines every other screen.
Fix
Replace the Deworming capture. For modules without captures, drop the device and show feature cards with the photo as a header, marked "Screens coming soon".
H2.4

Names and summaries follow the system, not the reader

1 Cosmetic
Seen
"Notes Module", "Helpdesk Module", "Hospital Information Management System · App". Summaries are auto-trimmed intros and end mid-sentence.
Fix
Drop "Module", shorten HIMS to "Hospital", and write a 12-word tagline per module.

H3User control and freedomCan people undo, leave and share?

H3.1

An open card cannot be shared or bookmarked

2 Minor
Seen
The open panel is page state only. Copying the URL shares the top of the home page. The only shareable deep link is the separate module page.
Fix
Reflect the open card in the hash (#housing) and reopen it on load, scrolled into view.
H3.2

Closing waits for the animation

1 Cosmetic
Seen
✕ or Esc un-draws the border (0.55 s) before the panel fades (0.28 s). The panel is unclickable meanwhile, so a quick close-then-reopen feels sluggish.
Fix
Run the un-draw and fade together, total under 0.35 s. Opening can stay slow and lovely; closing should be quick.

H4Consistency and standardsSame thing, same look, same words?

H4.1

The action green fails WCAG contrast

3 Major
Seen
White on #37BD69 is 2.43:1 on every "Book a walkthrough" and "Open the full tour" button, against a 4.5:1 minimum. Green eyebrows (#2A9D55) are 3.47:1.
Fix
Use #27864B (4.57:1) for button fills and green text; keep #37BD69 and the gradient for borders and accents, where 3:1 is enough.
H4.2

Numbers contradict each other

2 Minor
Seen
Cards are numbered 01 to 32, where 03 is Collection, yet the Getting Started section is labelled "Module 03". The "All 32" chip prints the total the team agreed not to quote until the module count is settled.
Fix
Remove card numbers (they carry no meaning for prospects), drop "Module 03", and show "All" without a count until the module count is settled.
H4.3

Two different views for the same module

2 Minor
Seen
The in-place panel and the full module page present the same content with different layouts, behaviours and phone handling.
Fix
Make the module page render the panel component at full width, so there is one pattern to learn and maintain.
H4.4

Hero chips and filter chips look unrelated

1 Cosmetic
Seen
The hero uses spaced capitals on glass; the filters use sentence case pills with counts. Both mean the same five areas.
Fix
Use sentence case and the same pill shape in both places.

H5Error preventionCan the main action fail?

H5.1

Booking can fail without the visitor knowing

3 Major
Seen
All three "Book a walkthrough" buttons open a mailto: link. On a work laptop with no mail app, or inside a shared page, nothing happens. The address is shown as text only in the bottom banner.
Fix
Link to a short form or scheduler with a confirmation state. Until then, show the address with a copy button beside every booking button.

H6Recognition rather than recallCan people tell things apart at a glance?

H6.1

32 cards share two photos

2 Minor
Seen
Every card alternates the marmoset and the tiger cub. The earlier thumbnails showed each module's screen; now only the title distinguishes one card from the next, and the summary is cut at two lines.
Fix
Commission one photo per area (five images) so the grid is readable by colour and subject, and let the icon carry the module.
H6.2

Search lives only in the hero

2 Minor
Seen
Once a visitor scrolls past the banner or opens a module page, the only way to find something is to scroll or go back.
Fix
Add a compact search to the sticky header (opened with /), with synonyms such as "death" for Mortality and "hospital" for HIMS.
H6.3

Filters wrap to a lonely second row

1 Cosmetic
Seen
At 1440px "Operations & Oversight" drops to a row of its own, which makes it look secondary.
Fix
Shorten labels (Records, Animals, Medical, Lifecycle, Operations) so all six fit on one row.

H7Flexibility and efficiency of useIs it quick for someone in a hurry?

H7.1

The page is very long

2 Minor
Seen
11,600px on desktop, most of it the 32-card grid. On a phone the hero alone fills the first screen, with five stacked chips at 32px (under the 44px touch target).
Fix
Show one row per area with "Show all 7", and put the phone chips in one scrolling row at 44px.
H7.2

Features cannot be scanned all at once

1 Cosmetic
Seen
Only the selected feature shows its description, and each selection replays a one-second device rise.
Fix
Add a "Show all descriptions" toggle, and play the rise only on the first view per panel.
H7.3

Touch visitors miss the signature effect

1 Cosmetic
Seen
The drawn border and lift are hover effects. On phones a card simply opens.
Fix
Play the border draw on tap-and-hold or on open (it already runs on open), and add a subtle press state.

H8Aesthetic and minimalist designIs anything competing for attention?

H8.1

Seven motion effects compete

2 Minor
Seen
Hero parallax, card fade-up, card border draw, chip border draw, chip lift and wash, panel border draw, and a device rise on every feature. Individually tasteful; together they make the page feel busy.
Fix
Keep the gradient border as the one signature. Retire the chip border draw (the wash is enough), and limit the device rise to first view.
H8.2

Twenty cards in three sections

2 Minor
Seen
Objective (4 cards), What is Antz (6 cards), Getting Started (6 list items) and the stepper (4 steps) all use the same white-card treatment in a row.
Fix
Merge Objective into What is Antz as three outcomes, and fold Getting Started's six features into the four-step stepper.

H9Help users recognise, diagnose and recover from errors

H9.1

Unknown links fail silently

1 Cosmetic
Seen
A mistyped or retired module link redirects to the top of the home page without explanation. The search empty state, by contrast, is good: it names the query and suggests alternatives.
Fix
Show a short "That module has moved" note above the cards, with the closest match.

H10Help and documentation

H10.1

The FAQ skips the buyer's first questions

2 Minor
Seen
Five answers cover devices, roles and sites. Nothing on pricing, time to go live, migrating existing records, training, offline use or where data is hosted.
Fix
Ask sales for the ten most common questions and add approved answers, grouped as Product, Rollout and Security.
Recommendations

Suggestions beyond the fixes

These go further than individual findings. Each one makes the tour easier to follow or more memorable without adding weight.

1. Lead with one story

Open the page, after the banner, with a single scenario that crosses modules: "Moving an animal between sites" through Transfer, Approvals, Security and Housing. Real screens exist for every step.

Prospects remember stories, not catalogues. The grid then becomes the reference.

2. Choose your role

Four small cards (Curator, Vet, Keeper, Director) that filter the grid to the six modules each role uses daily.

Turns 32 choices into six, in the visitor's own terms.

3. One module, two surfaces

Merge each App and Web pair, and the three treatment modules, behind an App | Web switch inside the panel.

Takes the grid from 32 cards to about 24 and shows the two-surface story visually.

4. A proof strip

Under the banner: number of sites, animals and species managed on Antz, and one customer quote. Only real figures from Antz.

The page currently has claims but no evidence.

5. Photography with a job

One graded photo per area, subjects looking into the page, with space for the icon. Reuse the same five across hero chips, filters and cards.

Colour and subject become navigation cues.

6. A motion budget

Write it down: one signature (the gradient border), one entrance (the fade-up), and quick exits. Anything new has to replace something.

Keeps the craft visible without the page feeling busy.
